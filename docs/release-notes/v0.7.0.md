# Musa CAD v0.7.0

The **modify and dimension-editing** release: TRIM and EXTEND with AutoCAD's modes, by a
path, on ellipses and construction lines, and showing what a pick would do; FILLET on
parallel lines and polylines; LENGTHEN, JOIN and PEDIT brought to AutoCAD's options; new
tools from OVERKILL to BLEND; DIM, QDIM and the commands that edit dimensions already
drawn; layers at the command line; the system clipboard; and crash reports.

> **Linux, macOS and Windows.** The Linux AppImage, the Flatpak bundle and the macOS disk
> image shipped with the tag; the Windows installer was added after verification on real
> Windows hardware. It is built from `main` one commit past the tag, a Windows-only fix: a
> crash report for an exception nothing caught names the exception, as it does on Linux.
> Everything else is the tagged source. A v0.6.0 installation updates to it from the Update
> window.

If you are coming from v0.6.0, the native format moved from v38 to v39 (a dimension's text
angle, an arc-length dimension's leader, oblique extension lines): v0.6.0 files open
unchanged, files saved by v0.7.0 need v0.7.0 or newer.

---

## TRIM and EXTEND as AutoCAD has them (#48)

`[cuTting edges/Crossing/mOde/Project/eRase]` with **Quick** mode (every object cuts; an
object with nothing to trim it to is deleted) and **Standard** (choose the cutting edges
first). Press on empty space and drag across the objects to trim each one where the path
crosses it -- or click twice for a straight fence, or type **Fence** points -- all in one
undo step. **Crossing** trims the objects a window crosses, **Edge Extend** lets an edge
cut along its extension, **eRase** erases without leaving the command, and **Shift** swaps
trim and extend for one pick. They work on ellipses and elliptical arcs, construction lines
and rays, and a polyline's arc end too.

Hovering an object shows what the pick would do: the part TRIM would cut away, the length
EXTEND would add, and -- at FILLET's and CHAMFER's second prompt -- both objects as they
would be with the arc or the bevel (Shift: the sharp corner).

## Modify, the rest of it (#49, #52, #55)

- **FILLET** joins two parallel lines with a half circle, and a line with a polyline's end
  segment (or two polylines) into one polyline with the corner rounded.
- **LENGTHEN** reports the length of what you pick, takes **DYnamic** and an arc's
  **Angle**, goes from one object to the next with **Undo**, and works on polylines and
  elliptical arcs.
- **JOIN** takes a source object or a window; lines along one line, arcs on one circle and
  elliptical arcs on one ellipse become one object each, and **cLose** makes the circle or
  the ellipse.
- **PEDIT** **Fit** puts two arcs on each segment, smooth at every vertex; **Multiple**
  edits every polyline selected, with a fuzz distance for Join.
- **DIVIDE** and **MEASURE** place a **Block** at the marks, turned to the curve if asked.
- New: **REVERSE**, **OVERKILL** (duplicates deleted, overlapping lines made one),
  **COPYTOLAYER**, **CHPROP**, **BLEND** (a spline between two ends, Tangent or Smooth) and
  **REDO** / **MREDO** as commands.

## Layers (#55, #69)

**LAYOFF**, **LAYFRZ**, **LAYLCK**, **LAYULK**, **LAYMCUR**, **LAYCUR**, **LAYMCH**,
**LAYISO** / **LAYUNISO**, **LAYON** and **LAYTHW**, several of them on the Home tab's Layers
panel. **LAYER** (`LA`) opens the Layer Properties Manager and **-LAYER** works on layers at
the command line: list, make, set, add, rename, and switch them on, off, frozen or locked by
name and wild card, with their colour, linetype and lineweight.

## Draw (#39, #40, #45)

**TRACE**, **SOLID**, **BOUNDARY** (closed polylines round the area a hatch would fill),
**CENTERMARK** and **CENTERLINE**. ELLIPSE previews its Rotation step and **PELLIPSE** 1
draws ellipses as polylines of arcs; REVCLOUD draws the **Calligraphy** style and remembers
its type.

## Dimensions (#56, #57, #60)

- **DIM** places one dimension after another, each suited to what you pick -- a line
  aligned (a second line: the angle between them), a circle by its diameter, an arc by its
  radius -- previewing it as you hover, with **aliGn**, **Distribute** and **Layer**
  (DIMLAYER) for dimensions already drawn.
- **QDIM** dimensions the selected geometry at once: a chain, staggered, from a baseline,
  ordinates, radii or diameters.
- **DIMEDIT** and **DIMTEDIT** put text home, replace it, rotate it, slide it left or right,
  or slant a dimension's extension lines (**Oblique**); **DIMSPACE** spaces parallel
  dimensions; **DIMCENTER** marks a centre; **DIMOVERRIDE** gives dimensions settings of
  their own.
- **Angle** at every dimension's placement prompt; DIMARC's **Partial** and **Leader**;
  DIMCONTINUE and DIMBASELINE's **Select** and **Undo** (and an **Offset** for the
  baseline), chaining angular and ordinate dimensions too.

## Also

- **The system clipboard**: Ctrl+C and Ctrl+X put the objects on it as Musa CAD objects and
  as a picture; Ctrl+V pastes them into another window, and pastes images and text other
  programs copy. **COPYBASE**, **CUTBASE** and **PASTEORIG** too.
- **ORTHO**, **SNAP** and **GRID** at the command line.
- **Crash reports**: if Musa CAD stops unexpectedly, the next start shows a report to save
  and attach to an issue; **Save Bug Report…** writes one at any time.
- Building from source with GCC takes half the memory it did.

## Fixed

- The drawing area starts on NVIDIA graphics under Wayland instead of saying OpenGL is
  missing (#82) -- thanks to @devgarden-de for tracing it.
- PURGE kept removing a layer used only inside a block definition, and removing a layer
  left block contents and undo on the wrong layers; both are fixed.
- A command's own **Undo** (TRIM, LENGTHEN, FILLET, DIM, DIMCONTINUE and the rest) no longer
  takes back what came before the command when one of its steps changed nothing.
- Picking an ellipse no longer misses it near half its outline, moving or pasting one no
  longer leaves copies in the preview, and EXTEND on an arc stops where a curved boundary
  really is.
