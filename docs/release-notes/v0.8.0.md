# Musa CAD v0.8.0

The **drawing-content** release: draw order, transparency on single objects and ByBlock;
multilines, spline-fit polylines and splines that keep their fit points; associative
hatches and dimensions; multileaders with styles; the Block Editor; data links and fields;
plot preview and PUBLISH; and Express Tools from TEXTMASK to BLOCKREPLACE.

> **Linux first.** The Linux AppImage and the Flatpak bundle ship with the tag (the macOS
> disk image too, the command line only). The Windows installer follows once it has been
> verified on real Windows hardware, added to this same release; a v0.7.0 installation
> then updates to it from the Update window.

If you are coming from v0.7.0, the native format moved from v39 to v45 (multilines, a
fitted polyline's frame, a spline's fit points, a multileader's style, the draw order, an
object's own transparency and ByBlock): v0.7.0 files open unchanged, files saved by v0.8.0
need v0.8.0 or newer.

---

## Draw order, transparency and ByBlock (#55, #68, #69)

Every object has its place in the draw order: a new one goes over the rest, a copy over
its source, an edit stays where it was. **DRAWORDER** (`DR`) brings objects to the front,
sends them to the back, or puts them above or under others; **TEXTTOFRONT** and
**HATCHTOBACK** do the same for the text, dimensions and leaders, or for the hatches. Fills,
hatches, wipeouts and images hide what is under them, on screen and on paper.

An object's own **transparency** (the Properties palette, **CETRANSPARENCY**) beside the
layer's, and **ByBlock** colour, linetype and lineweight: an object in a block drawn in the
block reference's. All of it is kept in the drawing and in DXF.

## Drawing (#39, #40, #45, #52)

- **MLINE**, **MLSTYLE** and **MLEDIT**: multilines with their styles, caps, fill and
  joints; crosses, tees, corners, cuts and welds.
- **PEDIT Spline** makes a spline-fit polyline as AutoCAD does, keeping its frame for the
  grips, the vertex editor and **Decurve**; the vertex editor's **Tangent** steers **Fit**.
- **SPLINE** keeps the fit points it was drawn through (grips on them, refitted as one
  moves), with start and end **Tangency** and **toLerance**; **Object** turns a spline-fit
  polyline into the same curve.
- **WIPEOUT** is an object of its own and reads and writes AutoCAD's WIPEOUT in DXF;
  **REVCLOUD Modify**; point display (PDMODE, PDSIZE); **isometric drafting**.

## Hatches (#41)

Associative hatches that follow their boundaries, AutoCAD's standard patterns and .pat
files, user-defined hatching, **GRADIENT**, the hatch gallery and swatches, `-HATCH`'s
options, and **HATCHEDIT** -- Properties, boundaries added and removed, the origin,
recreated boundaries, separate hatches.

## Dimensions (#58, #59, #60)

**Associative dimensions** that follow the objects they measure, **DIMREASSOCIATE** point
by point and the dimension grip menus; the Dimension Style Manager's **Tolerances**,
alternate units, zero suppression and text placement; **DIMBREAK**, **DIMJOGLINE**,
**DIMINSPECT**, and QDIM's datumPoint and Edit.

## Multileaders and leaders (#61)

**MLEADER** with **MLEADERSTYLE** (a style's multileaders follow it), **MLEADERALIGN**,
**MLEADEREDIT** and spline leaders; **QLEADER** as AutoCAD has it, with its settings.

## Blocks and attributes (#75)

The **Block Editor** (BEDIT, BCLOSE, BSAVE), **BLOCKREPLACE**, **ATTSYNC**, **BASE** and
drawings inserted as blocks, -INSERT's options, block units, and attribute values kept by
tag when a block is redefined.

## Text, fields, tables and data (#43, #44, #62, #71)

MTEXT's corner options and `-MTEXT`; the **Field** and **Geometric Tolerance** windows;
**data links** to CSV and Excel workbooks (with Excel's number formats), **DATALINKUPDATE**
and **UPDATEFIELD**; **DATAEXTRACTION** and **TABLEEXPORT**.

## Layers (#55, #69)

**LAYERSTATE** and the Layer States Manager (import and export of .las files), layer group
and property filters, **LAYERP**, **LAYMRG**, **LAYDEL** and **LAYWALK**, layers that are
not plotted, descriptions, transparency and the Plot Style column.

## Output (#74, #81)

**PREVIEW**, **EXPORTPDF** and **PUBLISH**, the **Page Setup Manager** with a page setup for
each layout, and plotting in inches.

## Drafting aids and units (#54, #65, #81)

Dynamic Input's **pointer input** and option list, **DSETTINGS**, GRID and LIMITS, grip
dimensions and hot, shared and several grips; feet, inches and fractions at every prompt,
and the drawing's units in DXF.

## The interface (#72, #73, #78)

The **View Manager** with view categories and boundaries, the navigation bar and the UCS
icon; **tool palettes**, **workspaces**, keyboard shortcuts and command aliases of your
own; the Quick Access Toolbar's drop-down; ribbon tabs and panels shown or hidden,
minimized, and searched.

## Express Tools (#77)

TEXTMASK, TXTEXP, TCOUNT, TEXTFIT, BURST, GATTE, EXTRIM, NCOPY, MOCORO, FLATTEN, VPSYNC,
VPSCALE, XLIST, ATTOUT and ATTIN, MPEDIT, DIMEX and DIMIM, TFRAMES.

## Fixed

- JOIN chains into a polyline only ends that meet, and keeps every piece of the chain.
- A hatch's Undo, PEDIT Multiple's Undo and FILLET with wide polylines keep what they
  should.
- The Plot window and the Page Setup Manager list the page setups saved with the drawing.
- DIMCONTINUE and DIMBASELINE use DIMLAYER's layer; LAYUNISO works after switching drawing
  tabs.
- Ctrl+C then Ctrl+V pastes what was just copied, whatever was on the clipboard before.
