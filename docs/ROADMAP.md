# Musa CAD roadmap

Musa CAD focuses on 2D drafting, drawing exchange and automation. The
[issue tracker](https://github.com/MusaCAD/MusaCAD/issues) holds current work and
its discussion. Issue scope can be partly implemented; an open issue does not mean
every feature in its title is missing.

## Areas of ongoing work

- [Annotation scaling](https://github.com/MusaCAD/MusaCAD/issues/76),
  [text editing](https://github.com/MusaCAD/MusaCAD/issues/43), and
  [annotation dialogs](https://github.com/MusaCAD/MusaCAD/issues/62).
- [Block editing](https://github.com/MusaCAD/MusaCAD/issues/75),
  [arrays](https://github.com/MusaCAD/MusaCAD/issues/51), and
  [parametric constraints](https://github.com/MusaCAD/MusaCAD/issues/70).
- [Plotting and publishing](https://github.com/MusaCAD/MusaCAD/issues/74),
  [fields and data links](https://github.com/MusaCAD/MusaCAD/issues/71), and
  [workspace customization](https://github.com/MusaCAD/MusaCAD/issues/73).
- [macOS testing and distribution](https://github.com/MusaCAD/MusaCAD/issues/2).

These are areas for contribution, not a release schedule. Read each issue for its
current scope and status. [Command status](COMMANDS.md) and
[settings status](AUTOCAD_CONFIG.md) describe individual features.

## Releases and documentation

Use the [release notes](https://github.com/MusaCAD/MusaCAD/releases) to check what
has shipped and the [wiki](https://wiki.musacad.org/) for instructions. Linux and
Windows packages are available for 0.9.0. macOS has a separate experimental build.
Flathub is an active distribution channel.
